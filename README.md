# Lake Harmony Trip Plan

**Destination:** Sundrift Escape, Lake Harmony, PA (Poconos)
**Origin:** Brambleton, VA
**Duration:** Saturday morning to Monday 10 AM checkout
**Drive:** ~4 hours, ~240 miles via I-81 N → I-78 E → I-476 N

---

## On the Way (Saturday Morning Stop)

Three options, all add ~2 hours to the drive:

| Option | Distance from Brambleton | Stop Duration | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hershey's Chocolate World, Hershey PA | ~2.5 hr in | ~2 hr | Family, food, Adi |
| Cabela's Hamburg, PA | ~2.75 hr in | ~45 min | Quick novelty browse |
| Crystal Cave, Kutztown PA | ~2.75 hr in | ~1.5 hr | Geology, guided tour |

**Recommended:** Hershey. Best combination of food, activity, and family appeal.

---

## Saturday — Arrival Day

- **8:00 AM** — Depart Brambleton
- **10:30 AM – 1:00 PM** — Hershey's Chocolate World tour, tasting, lunch in town
- **3:30 PM** — Arrive Lake Harmony area
- **4:00 PM** — Check in at Sundrift Escape
- **Afternoon** — Settle in, golf cart tour around Split Rock
- **Evening** — Grill dinner, fire pit, hot tub, sauna
- **Night** — Game room and home theater for Adi

---

## Sunday — Full Day

### Morning: Hickory Run State Park (10 min away)

- **Boulder Field**: 16 acre National Natural Landmark. Unique geological site, easy access, high wow factor for Adi.
- **Hawk Falls Trail**: 1.2 mi out and back to a 25 ft waterfall.
- **Total time:** ~3 hours

### Afternoon: Two Options

| Option | Vibe | Cost | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lake Harmony Boat Club (kayak, paddleboard, swim) | Lower energy, lake focused | $ | **Primary pick** |
| Camelback Mountain Adventures (mountain coaster, treetop course) | High energy, theme park style | $$$ | If Adi is restless |

### Evening at Property

- Sauna and massage chair for the adults
- Movie theater plus arcade for Adi
- Fire pit wind down

---

## Monday — Departure

- Slow breakfast at the house
- **10:00 AM** — Checkout
- **Optional stop**: Pocono Premium Outlets, Tannersville (20 min south, adds ~1 hour)
- **3:00 PM** — Home in Brambleton
